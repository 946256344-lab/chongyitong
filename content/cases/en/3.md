## IV. Prognosis Reference

Based on published evidence for canine diabetes mellitus:

- **Uncomplicated DM with good management**: ~80% of dogs achieve stable glucose regulation within 1–3 months of insulin initiation
- **Survival after diagnosis**: Median 2–3 years with active management; many dogs live 4–5+ years post-diagnosis
- **Cataracts**: ~75% of diabetic dogs develop cataracts within 12 months — discuss prophylactic osmotic eye drops and surgical options with your vet proactively
- **Concurrent disease risk**: Hypothyroidism, hyperadrenocorticism (Cushing's), and UTI are common in diabetic dogs and cause insulin resistance — screen every 6 months
- **Remission**: Unlike cats, spontaneous insulin remission is rare in dogs; lifelong therapy is expected
