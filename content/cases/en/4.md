## IV. Prognosis Reference

Based on published evidence for feline CKD:

- **IRIS Stage 2 CKD with treatment**: Median survival 1,150–1,500 days (~3–4 years) from diagnosis
- **IRIS Stage 3 CKD with treatment**: Median survival 250–350 days from diagnosis; varies greatly with complication management
- **Renal diet effect**: Strong evidence that phosphorus-restricted diet extends survival 2–3× compared to regular food in Stage 2–3
- **Uncontrolled hypertension**: BP >180 mmHg significantly increases risk of acute blindness (retinal detachment) and stroke
- **Proteinuria (UPC >0.4)**: Associated with 3× faster progression rate — monitoring and treatment if UPC rises is important

> Whiskey's current clinical status is actually encouraging for a 14-year-old cat with CKD — he is compensated, eating, and interactive. The goal of treatment is not to cure CKD but to slow it down significantly. With a renal diet, blood pressure control, and phosphorus management, many cats at this stage maintain excellent quality of life for 2–3+ years.
