## IV. Prognosis Reference

Based on published HCM research in cats:

- **Asymptomatic HCM (Stage B1/B2)**: Median survival exceeds **5 years**; some cats live their full natural lives without progressing to heart failure
- **Congestive heart failure (Stage C, pulmonary edema present)**: Median survival approximately **6–18 months**, requiring urgent hospitalization and long-term diuretic maintenance
- **Aortic thromboembolism (ATE)**: One of the most dangerous complications; occurs in approximately 12–17% of HCM cats; requires immediate emergency care

> Key prognostic factors: rate of left atrial enlargement, ATE occurrence, and monitoring consistency. This cat was caught at a relatively early stage — regular follow-up is the single most important intervention right now.
